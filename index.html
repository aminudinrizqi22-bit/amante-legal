Amante

A personal AI assistant that remembers what matters to one person and helps them keep track of their own day.

What it is

Amante is a private project, built and used by its developer for personal use. It is not a public service and is not offered to other people.

What it does

  • Keeps notes and memories from conversations with its owner.
  • Tracks the owner's tasks and goals and sends reminders.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar events and can add or change them when asked.
  • Searches the owner's Gmail inbox, read-only, to answer questions such as "did the invoice arrive?"

Why it asks for Google access

Calendar and Gmail access is requested only so the owner can ask Amante about their own calendar and email. Amante asks for two permissions: view and edit calendar events, and read Gmail messages. It cannot send, delete or change email. The owner can remove this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
